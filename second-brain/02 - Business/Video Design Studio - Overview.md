# Video Design Studio

Motion Graphics, Video Editing, Product Videos — run by [[Javad Gougerdie]]. Rate: **$25.00/hr**.

> Good video doesn't just show something. It makes you see it differently.

That's what we do at Video Design Studio.

A product can be difficult to explain. A technology can be difficult to understand. A brand can have a lot to say — and still say nothing.

**We turn those ideas into stories.**

Sometimes that means a cinematic product film. Sometimes it's motion graphics that make a complex idea suddenly simple. Sometimes it's typography, rhythm, and sound doing almost all the talking.

We work with B2B companies, technology teams, product businesses and brands — taking whatever you have, from raw footage and screen recordings to a script, presentation, or unfinished idea, and finding the story inside it.

The craft is in the details: the first frame, the pace of the edit, the movement of type, the moment a product is revealed, the sound that makes a transition feel right.

We don't believe in motion for motion's sake. Every frame has a job. And when the video ends, the viewer should know what they saw, why it matters, and — ideally — remember it.
